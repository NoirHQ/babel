# Ziggurat Testnet

The Ziggurat Testnet currently operates locally as a parachain. It is not yet live on the Polkadot testnet, Paseo, but will be launched there soon. You can experience Babel’s core features through Ziggurat.

## Connect Wallet

On the Ziggurat interface, click the Connect button at the top right. A list of supported wallets will appear, currently including Polkadot, Ethereum, and Cosmos.
Supported wallet types:

- Polkadot: polkadot{.js}, SubWallet
- Ethereum: any wallet that supports EIP-6963
- Cosmos: Keplr

<img src="../../images/connect.png" alt="connect" width="50%"/>

## Request Faucet

1. Each address can receive 1 Zig per day using the faucet.
2. Enter your Polkadot, Ethereum, or Cosmos address in the To field.
3. Complete the reCAPTCHA, then click Get some ZIGs.

<img src="../../images/faucet.png" alt="faucet" width="50%"/>

## Send Asset

Enter the asset type, amount, and address to send, then click Send.

<img src="../../images/send.png" alt="send" width="50%"/>

## Swap Asset

1. Select the asset to sell and the asset to buy.
2. Enter the target amount for either the sell or buy quantity, and the corresponding amount will be automatically filled based on the current price.
3. Once both the sell and buy quantities are entered, click Swap to execute.
