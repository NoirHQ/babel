# Ziggurat Testnet

- A testnet operating on the Babel, functioning as a parachain with the Polkadot testnet Paseo as its relay chain.
- Experience Babel’s core features through the Ziggurat application.

## Connect Wallet

- On the Ziggurat interface, click the Connect button at the top right. A list of supported wallets will appear, currently including Polkadot, Ethereum, and Cosmos.
- Supported wallet types:
  - Polkadot: polkadot{.js}, SubWallet
  - Ethereum: any wallet that supports EIP-6963
  - Cosmos: Keplr

<img src="../../images/connect.png" alt="connect" width="50%"/>

## Request Faucet

- Each address can receive 1 Zig per day using the faucet.
- Enter your Polkadot, Ethereum, or Cosmos address in the To field.
- Complete the reCAPTCHA, then click Get some ZIGs.

<img src="../../images/faucet.png" alt="faucet" width="50%"/>

## Send Coin

- Enter the asset type, amount, and address to send, then click Send.

<img src="../../images/send.png" alt="send" width="50%"/>

## Swap Coin

- Select the asset to sell and the asset to buy.
- Enter the target amount for either the sell or buy quantity, and the corresponding amount will be automatically filled based on the current price.
- Once both the sell and buy quantities are entered, click Swap to execute.
